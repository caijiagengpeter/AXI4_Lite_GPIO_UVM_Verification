AXI4-Lite GPIO Functional Coverage Plan
=======================================

Baseline: docs/verification_plan.txt v1.0, Section 14
C3.4 state: C3.3 is frozen at v4.0-c3.3-baseline. Checker-verified alias,
AWADDR[1], W1C timing and v4 payload tests are added without DUT changes,
exclusions, deleted bins, or cross-parameter merging. Measured results and the
draft gap review are in docs/v4_c3_4_coverage_gap_closure.md; machine-readable
triage is in manifests/c3_4_coverage_gap_triage.json.

Field definitions
-----------------
Coverage ID          Stable identifier for the coverage item.
Feature/Requirement  Requirement or feature being observed.
Coverpoint/Cross      Planned sampling item or cross.
Bins/Intent           Reachable scenarios that must be distinguished.
Goal                  Closure target from the frozen plan.
Status                PLANNED until implementation and reviewed results exist.
Notes                 Sampling constraints, exclusions, or dependencies.

Closure policy
--------------
The frozen plan's initial target is at least 95% overall functional coverage
and 100% on reachable critical register, WSTRB, AW/W-ordering, W1C, and
interrupt-polarity bins. Any exclusion, unreachable bin, or goal adjustment
requires review and a recorded rationale. C3.4 reaches 99.18% on the expanded
default GPIO functional coverage object while retaining all bins. The response
latency immediate bin remains 0-hit and the W1C cross remains 6/8.
SPEC-MASK-001, code-coverage waiver approval, those timing gaps, and final
sign-off remain open.
